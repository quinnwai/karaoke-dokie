# Round 6A design: where production differs on purpose

`setlist-round6a-README.md` and `setlist-round6a.html` are the design as it was handed over (QWW-57). They are kept as they
were. Round 6A reskins the Round 5 tuner (`docs/setlist-round5/`, which this follows for anything 6A does not mention) into the
app's "night out" look. The screen is `src/components/setlist/`; this lists where it differs from them, and why. The Round 5
list still applies to the rows it names (the engine, rank order in the DOM, the song page, the test hook).

Quinn-Wai answered the open questions on 11 Nov (QWW-46 and QWW-57); the decisions are numbered as in QWW-57.

| 6A says | Production does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| §3.3: a device frame, 390×844 as a mock, filling the viewport at 430 px wide or less | The tuner renders inside `Tv` (`src/components/Tv.tsx`), the TV the title and mic steps use: one frame for every step, no second bezel. On a phone (up to 430 px) `Tv` takes 6A's numbers (page padding 14 10 12, bezel padding 9 9 0, radii 30 and 22, a 34 px chin with the LED) and fills the viewport; the bottom padding clears the home bar. On a wider window it is the same frame, phone-shaped: 390 px wide and min(844 px, the window less 32 px) tall, centred, on every step (the title, the mic and note steps and the tuner), with no column inside it. The text that scales with the window width stops growing at a phone's width (`--vw` is `min(1vw, 4.3px)`) | Decision 2 (it reverses the PR #4 note that took the frame away). That tightens the earlier steps by a few px on phones, which is the point. `Tv` already draws the scanlines and the inner shadow, so the tuner draws neither. The phone-shaped desktop frame is Quinn-Wai's review (QWW-57, R2): "phone shaped"; 6A's own mock is 390×844 |
| §3.1: the tokens | The same values, scoped to the tuner as `--sl-*` custom properties; the earlier steps keep theirs. The bezel and screen colours stay the TV's own | Round 5 scoped its own tokens the same way |
| §3.1, §7, §11: off label `#6E6A78`, off end labels `#5E5A68` (3.7:1 and 2.9:1) | An off gear's label (the four gears and Era), its " · OFF" tag and its off end labels are `#8E8A99`, 4.8:1 on the strip | Decision 5 (§13 left it to a fix or a sign-off) |
| §3.2: fonts from Google Fonts | Inter 400, 500, 600, 700 from `@fontsource/inter`, and a 2 KB Noto Sans JP 700 subset of the four characters of the sign in `src/components/setlist/fonts/`, both served from this site. Barlow is gone | §13: self-host for production. The earlier steps keep the system font |
| §3.2: era button labels 10.5 px | The largest size that fits, up to 10.5 px, set by the Era panel's own width (container units, `cqw`) and not the window's: 9 px at 360 px wide, about 9.4 at 375, about 10 at 390 and 10.5 from about 405 px up, with -0.03em tracking (`.eraButton` in `sheet.module.css`). The 390 px desktop frame, which is 390 px wide whatever the window is, gets the size its own width needs | "Pre-’70s" is 40 px wide in Inter at 10.5 px with that tracking and its button is 35 to 42 px wide (a 360 px phone to the desktop frame), so it spilled over its button at the narrow ones. The size was keyed to the window until the adversarial review of #38 (finding 8) |
| §3.4, §13: the range and redo are static placeholders; the sign is right-aligned | The range is the singer's own: tap a note to re-sing that end; redo re-sings both ends. Both are 44 px tall to tap and look as 6A draws them. The Spotify logo (Connect, or the signed-in account with the name and Log out) is at the far right; the sign is centred on the row's own middle (a pixel's tolerance), not on the room between redo and the logo, and shrinks from 16 px down to 13 px to keep 8 px clear of redo and the logo, and is hidden, not moved, only when 13 px does not fit (the longest range on a 360 px phone), before anything else shrinks. The notes (text and dotted underline) and redo (border and text) turn `--pink-text` on hover and on keyboard focus | Round 5 had already wired them; decision 4. The centring and the hover are Quinn-Wai's review (QWW-57, R1 and R9): "center the karaoke sign based on absolute", "hover pink" |
| §4: rank column, 18 px wide, before the text | A 44 px slot holds the rank number and the ▶ on top of each other: ▶ · text · badge, 10 px apart. On a pointer device the number shows, and hovering the row (or focusing the ▶) swaps it for the ▶, the glyph alone with no ring or circle in any state; a ▶ that cannot play yet is faded and disabled, and a preview that is loading, playing or paused shows its glyph without a hover. On a touch screen (`hover: none`) the ▶ stands in the number's place and the number is not drawn. The number is hidden from a screen reader (the `<ol>` says each position) and the rank is also in `data-rank`; the ▶ is 44 px to tap and never opens the song page. The ▶ is QWW-48's play button (#28), which `KaraokeApp` mounts in the slot: the slot's CSS hides the ring it draws, and the pink title it gives the playing row is not carried over (R12: titles end white), so the row that sounds is the one with ⏸ in its slot. A line under the list, over the credits link, names Deezer, as its API terms ask (#34's notes put it there) | Decision 3 first dropped the number for the ▶; Quinn-Wai's review (QWW-57, R3) brought the number back and asked for the ▶ "on hover … only the play button without the circle" |
| §4: filtered rows fade in place at the bottom of the list; rows sit in catalogue order | Rows are in rank order in the DOM (FLIP slides them); a row that leaves is removed | Round 5's `docs/setlist-round5/README.md`: a screen reader must read rank order |
| §4: the reason rules on mock scores | The same rules on the engine's places (percentiles among the songs that fit the singer): a null place skips its rule, so signed out "On repeat" and "Rediscover" never show; a song with no tempo falls through to "Solid pick"; "In your sweet spot" needs Difficulty on. `whyOf` is a pure function beside `toRow` (`src/lib/setlist/rows.ts`) | QWW-57. The rules themselves are not tuned (§13) |
| §4: the badge from a 34 / 67 cut on the song's Difficulty place | The badge is the song's own label (`match.difficulty`, the word the song page shows). A Maybe song (an estimated range) gets none | Medium places start at 33.3, so the cut would call some Medium songs Easy |
| §8: the Era count is the number of ranked songs | It is `SetlistResponse.total`: every song that fits the range and the picked eras, not the loaded page. "ERA · 1,204 songs", "ERA · 1 song" | QWW-57 |
| §2, §4, §12: the mock defaults (30 / 65 / 80 / 45, Era 90s, 00s, 10s) and their fixtures | The app's real defaults (`src/lib/setlist/defaults.ts`, QWW-47): Difficulty 0 and Popularity 100 on; History, Tempo and Era off; Era has no default picks. The §4 top-4 fixture and tests 16, 23, 24, 26 and 27 are rewritten for them and for the engine's real ranking of the 25 mock songs | The same as Round 5's first row |
| §1, §3.3: the sheet opens expanded unless `?mini=1` | A screen that would be left with fewer than 3 whole rows opens in mini (a 390×664, 412×780 and 375×553; a 390×844 still opens expanded), and where the expanded sheet cannot fit inside the screen at all (a 375×553) the handle keeps it mini. Expanded stays one drag up (`src/lib/setlist/fit.ts`, measured by `useSheetFit`) | Decision 1 |
| §13: row taps do nothing | Tapping a row opens the song page; the ▶ never does | The same as Round 5's row, and Quinn-Wai's PR #4 note |
| Not in 6A: History signed out, or Spotify unreachable | Drawn as an off gear (unlit art, dark knob, no reset button), with the Round 5 hint ("Connect Spotify to use your listening history", or the unreachable one) in place of the end labels, in `--text-3`. It stays disabled | QWW-57 |
| Not in 6A: the other states of the list | Loading, "Couldn’t load songs." with Try again, no songs in the picked eras, nothing in range, the library notes, Load more, the Connect note at the end and the credits icon are restyled in 6A's tokens like the empty state (15/500, `--text-3`) | QWW-57 |
| §2: `window.setlist` also has `viewModel()` | It has `getState`, `setState`, `ranking` and `songs`, as in Round 5, and only in the e2e build (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SETLIST_TEST_HOOK=1`) | The reference's `viewModel` is its view layer, which this build does not have |
| Reference: the invisible `<input type="range">` spans the strip body | It also starts 7 px before the track and ends 7 px after it, with a 14 px thumb (the fader cap) | So the native thumb's centre travels exactly the drawn track: a tap lands on the value the knob shows. Round 5 did the same with 9 px and 18 |
| §1: the metronome's period is unchanged from Round 5 | The period rounds the bpm first, `60 / round(50 + 1.5 × tempo)`, as the reference's script and §6 do. It differs from Round 5's by 0.01 or 0.02 s at a few tempos (1, 3, 5, 7, 15, 21, 25, 35, 49 and 63) and agrees at 0, 45, 50, 55 and 100, so test 28's `swing14` at 0.45 s holds either way | The reference wins for geometry and motion numbers (README §1) |
| §10: notes and bolts appear with a 180 ms opacity fade | The strip's notes and bolts fade; the tab icons' rays, which mirror them, switch without one | That is what the reference draws (`.fade` is on the strip's groups only) |
| §6, §10: a History note, a Popularity bolt or a tab ray that the value does not reach is hidden (`opacity: 0`) | It is drawn unlit (`#34313D`, full opacity) and lights when the value reaches it, on the same thresholds, (i + 0.5) × 100 / 7. A gear that is off is all unlit, as before. Each one says which it is in `data-lit="true"` or `"false"`. On the strip the colour fades from unlit to lit over 180 ms (the reference fades the opacity); the tab's rays switch without a fade, as above. On the History and Popularity tabs the glow (a 3 px blur in the gear's colour, as the other tabs have) is an SVG filter on the one group that holds the body and the lit rays, so an unlit ray has none (the adversarial review of #38, finding 3), and the tab's svg has `overflow: visible` so the glow is not clipped | Quinn-Wai's review of the preview (QWW-57, R7): "popularity should have the lines greyed out so it matches the style of a neon sign not turned on". The shape of the art no longer changes with the value |
| §5, §7, §8: a strip's and the Era panel's border is the gear's colour at 27% while the gear is on, the rail's grey while it is off | The rail's grey (`--sl-rail`) in every state and mode: on, off, disabled, expanded, mini. The selected tab in mini keeps its gear-colour border and glow. `--sl-gear-line` is gone | QWW-57, R5 |
| Not in 6A: Tempo's end labels in a narrow mini strip | Below a strip body of 144 px (the mini sheet at 360 to 374 px) Tempo's end labels ("Slow jams", "Upbeat tracks") tighten their tracking down to -0.03em and then their size (11.35 px at 360 px), so each stays on one line; wider, they are 6A's 12 px | At 360 px they were 138.5 px in a 129 px body, so they wrapped and the invisible slider sat 7 px off the drawn knob (found while fixing the History label). The other gears' end labels fit there |
| §9: the tab labels are 11 px | One size for all five labels, set by the tab bar's width (container units): 11 px from about 393 px up, 10.9 at 390, 10.3 at 375 and 9.7 at 360, never under 9.5 px, so the widest, "Popularity", keeps 3 px clear of its tab's border at every width | The adversarial review of #38 (finding 7): at 360 px it touched the border and at 375 px it ran into the padding |
| §5: History's label on the strip is "HISTORY" | "LISTENING HISTORY" (its tab still says "History", and the reset button's name is unchanged). The four labels set their type by the strip body's width: 11 px with 0.12em tracking on an expanded strip at 390 px (6A's own), down to 9.5 px with the tracking taken out, and to 9 px in the mini sheet at 360 px (where a strip's label and its reset button have no gap between them; the Era panel's header keeps its 8 px), so the longest label, "LISTENING HISTORY · OFF" signed in with History off, is never cut off and is one line in every state from 360 px up, which keeps the slider level with its knob (`.stripBody .eyebrow` in `sheet.module.css`). While History is disabled (signed out, or Spotify unreachable) the " · OFF" tag is not drawn, because its hint says why | QWW-57, R6 |
| §5: the sheet has a pink 1 px top edge and a pink glow above it | A neutral 1 px edge (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) and the dark shadow only: no pink along the top of the sheet | QWW-57, R8 |
| Not in 6A: a hover on a tab or an era button | On a pointer device (`@media (hover: hover)`) an idle mini tab gets a border in its gear's colour, and an unpicked Era button a pink border, in the expanded panel and in mini's Era view: no glow on either, which stays with the selected one. Nothing is left lit by a tap on a phone | QWW-57, R10 |
| §5: the gears' order is Difficulty, History, Popularity, Tempo | The strips and the tabs go Difficulty, Popularity, Tempo, Listening History, and the tabs end with Era: the gear that has to be unlocked is last. Mini still opens on Difficulty. The contract's `GEAR_IDS` is unchanged; the sheet's order is `SHEET_GEARS` in `gears.ts` | QWW-57, R11 |
| §4, §10: the #1 title has a pink lyric wipe that loops | Gone. Every title fills in once, left to right (about 0.6 s, a CSS transition of a copy of the title clipped to its filled part, so the text is in the page once), as its row scrolls into view, and ends the normal white, not pink. It plays once per visit, and a visit lasts until the singer's range changes (redo) or the page is reloaded: a row that scrolls out and back, is re-ranked, leaves when an era is untapped and comes back, or comes back after a song page and Back, does not replay it. `KaraokeApp` keeps the memory (`useFillMemory`), so it outlives the list. With reduced motion there is no fill | QWW-57, R12 |
| §4: the reason rules (sweet spot, popularity, history, then a tempo word) | A song that fits the singer's range only an octave away (`match.octave` `down` or `up`) says "Shifted down to fit range" or "Shifted up to fit range" and nothing else in its reason line, in place of "In your sweet spot", which a shifted song never says, and in place of the other reasons too (a second reason was cut off with an ellipsis on a phone: the adversarial review decided on the one reason). Its Easy, Medium or Hard badge stays; a song sung as written keeps its reasons. The song page is unchanged | QWW-57, R13, from QWW-58: Quinn-Wai chose the wording on 11 Nov. `SHIFT_REASON` and `whyOf` in `src/lib/setlist/rows.ts` |
| Not in 6A: a phone on its side | On a touch screen in landscape under 500 px tall (`(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse) and (orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 500px)`) a full-screen "Turn your phone upright" covers every step (`RotateNotice`, drawn by `Tv`). It is only drawn over the page: nothing underneath is unmounted, so turning the phone back finds the screen, the tuner's gears and sheet and a running mic step as they were. A desktop, a window taller than 500 px (a tablet) and a portrait phone never see it | The adversarial review of #38 (finding 1): a phone on its side is wider than 430 px, so it got the 390 px desktop frame at the window's height less 32 px, which held no songs (0 px of list at 640×360). The decision is chief-keef's, for Quinn-Wai to confirm |
| Not in 6A: the song page's Spotify link | "Find on Spotify" (signed out) and "Open in Spotify" end with an external-link icon (about 0.8 em, in the link's colour, hidden from a screen reader); the link's name is unchanged | QWW-57, R4 |

Also changed on the way (QWW-57, from the review of #34): History follows the engine's `libraryUnavailable` as well as the mode;
the list says "That’s every song that fits, for now." only when the engine's `nextOffset` is null; and the screen's defaults and
page size are imported from `src/lib/songs/setlistConfig.ts` instead of repeated.

Acceptance tests: `tests/e2e/setlist.spec.ts` runs README §12, Round 5's tests 1 to 21 with 6A's adjustments and the new tests
22 to 32, at 390×844, plus a run of the mode rule at 390×664, 412×780 and 375×553. Where a test names a mock default, it is
rewritten for the real one; the header of that file says how.

Quinn-Wai's review of the 6A preview (QWW-57, R1 to R13, 11 Nov) has one Playwright check per item, in three files:
`tests/e2e/setlist-frame.spec.ts` (R1 the sign's centre, R2 the phone-shaped frame on every step, R4 the link icon, R9 the
header's hover), `tests/e2e/setlist-rows.spec.ts` (R3 the ▶ and the rank number, R12 the title fill, R13 the shift in the reason line) and
`tests/e2e/setlist-sheet.spec.ts` (R5 borders, R6 the label, R7 unlit tubes, R8 the top edge, R10 hover borders, R11 the
order). Hover is always a pointer device's (`@media (hover: hover)`); a test with touch checks that nothing is left behind.

The adversarial review of #38 (chief-keef, 11 Nov) found eight more, each fixed with a test: `tests/e2e/landscape.spec.ts` (the
"Turn your phone upright" message), `tests/e2e/setlist-rows.spec.ts` (R12 per visit, R13 the one reason), `tests/e2e/setlist-tab-glow.spec.ts`
(R7, the glow of an unlit ray), `tests/e2e/setlist-frame.spec.ts` (R1, the sign shrinks before it hides) and `tests/e2e/setlist-sheet-fit.spec.ts` and
`setlist-sheet.spec.ts` (the strip label on one line, the tab labels and the era labels fit).
